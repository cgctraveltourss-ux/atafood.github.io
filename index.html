About Ata Food & Catering

Delivering reliability, safety, and delicious nutrition to workforce projects and corporate hubs across KSA.

Ata Food & Catering is a premier commercial food service and catering contractor operating within Saudi Arabia, with established logistical reach spanning Riyadh, Al Majma'ah, and Sudair Industrial City. We specialize in managing complex commercial catering workflows, tier-based workforce menu structuring, contract logistics, and customized food supply proposals for multinational project teams, industrial complexes, and corporate institutions.

Our commitment revolves around uncompromising food safety standards, punctual meal delivery schedules, and nutritionally balanced menus tailored to diverse workforce requirements. From large industrial camps to executive corporate dining, we bring excellence to every plate.
