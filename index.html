Who We Are

Trusted Industrial Food Logistics & Camp Catering

ATA FOOD & CATERING is a licensed Saudi commercial food services contractor established to support major infrastructure megaprojects, industrial hubs, and multinational corporate campuses across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

With central production kitchens and temperature-controlled logistics fleets in Riyadh and Sudair Industrial City, we manage end-to-end nutrition workflows — including menu engineering, procurement, hygienic cooking, hot-transport logistics, and on-site dining hall management.

Our Capabilities

Comprehensive Commercial Catering

Engineered to fulfill large-scale workforce needs, remote sites, and executive corporate environments.

Workforce & Camp Catering

High-volume daily meal services (breakfast, lunch, dinner, midnight shifts) tailored for construction workers, technicians, and operations personnel with calorically balanced, multicultural menus.

Executive Corporate Dining

Turnkey boardroom luncheons, staff cafeterias, and VIP executive meal programs curated with premium presentations, healthy dietary alternatives, and specialty cuisines.

Thermal-Fleet Hot Logistics

Modern fleet of insulated and refrigerated temperature-controlled vehicles ensuring hot meals arrive safe, piping hot, and exactly on timetable even at remote desert locations.

Dietary & Menu Engineering

Multicultural menu rotations (Arabian, Asian, Continental, Indian) curated by licensed culinary nutritionists to support health, employee satisfaction, and sustained productivity.

Corporate Events & Launches

Full hospitality support for corporate seminars, industrial plant inaugurations, safety milestone banquets, and annual corporate gatherings with professional service staff.

On-Site Kitchen Management

Complete management of client-owned camp dining facilities, mess halls, kitchen staff provisioning, equipment hygiene, and compliance auditing under strict SFDA criteria.
